KESTREL FINANCIAL GROUP PLC
Technology Committee — Minutes of the Meeting held on 19 March 2026
Location: Boardroom 2, 40 Gracechurch Street, London / hybrid
Chair: M. Halvorsen (Chief Operating Officer)

Present: M. Halvorsen (Chair), A. Renwick (CFO), S. Bello (CTO), P. Lindqvist (Head of Risk),
D. Achebe (Head of Operations), J. Moreau (General Counsel, joined for item 3).
Apologies: R. Tanaka (Non-Executive Director).

1. MINUTES OF THE PREVIOUS MEETING

The minutes of 12 February 2026 were approved without amendment. The Chair noted that two
actions from that meeting remain open: the disaster recovery test scheduled for Q1 has slipped
to April, and the vendor consolidation review has not yet been circulated.

2. RECONCILIATION PLATFORM — VENDOR SELECTION

S. Bello presented the outcome of the reconciliation platform selection. Three suppliers were
shortlisted. Northwind Systems Ltd scored highest on functional fit and was the only supplier
able to commit to a June go-live.

A. Renwick confirmed that the total two-year cost of the Northwind engagement is 180,000 GBP,
which sits within the envelope already approved in the FY26 technology budget. No further
capital approval is therefore required.

The Committee RESOLVED to approve the appointment of Northwind Systems Ltd for an initial
two-year term. J. Moreau was asked to finalise the contract for signature before the end of
March 2026.

D. Achebe asked whether the arrangement locked the Group in. M. Halvorsen confirmed that the
contract can be exited on 30 days notice at any point, so the commitment carries limited
downside. On that basis the Committee did not require a break-cost analysis.

ACTION 26/03/01 — J. Moreau to execute the Northwind Master Services Agreement by 31 March 2026.
ACTION 26/03/02 — S. Bello to confirm the implementation plan and go-live date to the Committee
by 10 April 2026.

3. PROJECT DELIVERY AND RESOURCING

S. Bello reported that implementation requires two full-time integration engineers from
1 April 2026. Only one is currently allocated; the second is expected to roll off the payments
migration in mid-May. The Committee noted the risk that this delays user acceptance testing.

The target go-live remains 30 June 2026. S. Bello advised that a four-week slip is possible if
the second engineer is not in place by 15 May 2026.

ACTION 26/03/03 — D. Achebe to identify contractor cover for the second engineer role and report
by 3 April 2026.

4. RISK AND SECURITY

P. Lindqvist raised that the security review of Northwind had not been completed at the time of
selection. The Committee agreed that the review should be completed before any Client data is
loaded into the platform, but did not make it a condition of signature.

P. Lindqvist further noted that the Group's standard position is that supplier data must remain
within the United Kingdom. He asked that this be confirmed in writing.

ACTION 26/03/04 — P. Lindqvist to circulate the completed security review by 17 April 2026.

5. DISASTER RECOVERY TEST

The annual disaster recovery test has been rescheduled to 22 April 2026. D. Achebe confirmed
that the test will cover the payments and custody systems but not the new reconciliation
platform, which will not be live at that date.

6. ANY OTHER BUSINESS

A. Renwick noted that technology spend is tracking 6% above plan for the year to date, driven
mainly by contractor rates. The Committee asked for a variance analysis at the next meeting.

The meeting closed at 11:40. The next meeting is scheduled for 16 April 2026.
