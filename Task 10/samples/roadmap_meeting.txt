Engineering Roadmap Review — Q2 Planning
20 March 2026, 14:00-15:30 WAT
Attending: Femi Adeyemi (Eng Manager), Chidera Nwosu (Platform Reliability),
Tolu Bakare (QA Lead), Sandra Okeke (Product), Ibrahim Musa (Data Platform)

Sandra opened by restating the Q2 commitment: ship the subscriptions feature by
15 June. She noted that two engineering weeks are already committed to reliability
follow-ups from the March incident and asked whether that number can be reduced.

Chidera confirmed the index fix shipped on 13 March and the runbook reordering is
done. The remaining items are the alert threshold change, the staging data seeding
job, and the checkout load tests.

Ibrahim pushed back on the staging seeding timeline. Sampling 10% of production
into staging requires a PII scrubbing step that Data Platform has not built, and
he estimated three weeks rather than the one week assumed in the incident report.
He proposed a narrower version: seed only the five largest tables, unsampled row
counts but synthetic values, which he can deliver in one week.

Tolu disagreed with narrowing it. Her position was that synthetic values will not
reproduce the planner behaviour that caused the incident, because the problem was
data distribution rather than row count alone. She argued the full scrubbing work
should be funded now, since this is the second incident in twelve months traced to
a staging-production gap.

Femi asked whether the load tests alone would have caught it. Tolu said probably
yes, and that load tests are one week of work, not three. Agreement formed around
sequencing: load tests first, then revisit staging seeding at the end of Q2.

DECISIONS
- Alert threshold change proceeds as planned, due 20 March, owner Femi.
- Checkout load tests are funded for Q2, one week, owner Tolu, due 10 April.
- Staging data seeding is deferred to Q3. Ibrahim will write a one-page scoping
  doc on the PII scrubbing work by 3 April so it can be estimated properly.
- Subscriptions ship date holds at 15 June.

Sandra raised customer communication separately. Support handled the incident well
but no proactive notice went out, and feedback shows customers want a public status
page. No owner was assigned. Femi said it belongs to Product rather than Engineering
and asked Sandra to bring a proposal to the next review.

Ibrahim noted before close that promo code validation is still running slower than
the rest of the checkout path even after the index fix, and that nobody has looked
at it. It was not added to the Q2 plan.
